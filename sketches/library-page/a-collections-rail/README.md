## Variant A: Collections rail

### Design stance
Browse first. The page mirrors how ScorePlay organises the club's media: a rail of collections on the left, the assets in a grid, search in the top bar.

### Key choices
- Layout: sidebar | collections rail (grouped: Match, Around the match, Creative, Photos / Game footage, Club posts, Photos) | grid | detail drawer.
- Search: the top-bar box runs TwelveLabs over the club's whole index; results replace the grid and each card opens AT the moment (window badge), not at the start of the file.
- Every card: ScorePlay name, length, collection, Searchable / Not indexed, and one action: **Make clip** (or **Index** when the video is not in TwelveLabs yet).
- Drawer: facts, the clip window on a bar, "Make it a" (reel + card / reel / card), Make clip. Make clip = the normal pipeline (Pegasus, rights, draft in the Inbox). Nothing publishes.
- Tokens: the producer desk's own light theme (same Inter, blue, grey ramp as the Inbox).

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: showing everything the club has and where it lives; obvious for a ScorePlay user.
- Weak at: search is a small box; a producer hunting for one moment has to know to type there.

### Best for
A producer who knows the shoot (pre-match, presser, dressing room) and wants to grab from a known shelf.
