## Variant B: Search first

### Design stance
Ask, don't browse. A big "Describe the moment you want…" box leads, with suggested queries; collections drop to a few group tabs below.

### Key choices
- Layout: sidebar | hero search + suggestion chips | group tabs (Everything, Match, Around the match, Creative, Photos) | grid | detail drawer.
- Search results, cards, drawer and Make clip behave exactly as in A.
- Fewer controls: no separate Video/Photos toggle (Photos is a tab).

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: the TwelveLabs story; the demo line is "type what you want, get the moment, one click to a draft".
- Weak at: individual collections (Pre-Match vs Post-Match) are one level down inside a group tab; less of a map of the library.

### Best for
A producer working fast from an idea ("Solanke goal", "away fans singing") rather than a known shelf.
