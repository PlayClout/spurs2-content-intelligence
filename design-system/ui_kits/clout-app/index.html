Mobile app — canonical 390×844 screens. Every screen is composed only from design-system components; no bespoke styling. The same compositions drive iOS and Android — platform differences are limited to safe-area insets, the 44/48px touch minimum, and dialog presentation (bottom sheet on mobile, centered modal at ≥768px). The Home and Pick'em screens are interactive: tap the tab bar, choose picks, submit to open the confirm sheet. Clout is free to play — no stakes, no wagering; eligibility is checked only at prize redemption.
